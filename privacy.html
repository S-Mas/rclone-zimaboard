Privacy policy

rclone-zimaboard is a personal backup tool used only by its author.

  • Data accessed: only files and folders the tool itself creates in the author's Google Drive (drive.file scope). It cannot read any other file.
  • Use: storing and restoring encrypted backups (restic). Content is encrypted on the author's server before upload; Google and third parties cannot read it.
  • Sharing: no data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. No analytics, no advertising.
  • Storage: the OAuth token is kept only on the author's home server.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Política de privacidad

Herramienta personal de su autor. Solo accede a los ficheros que crea en su propio Google Drive, los cifra antes de subirlos y no comparte, vende ni analiza ningún dato. El token se guarda solo en su servidor doméstico.
