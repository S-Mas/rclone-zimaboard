rclone-zimaboard

Personal backup tool. It runs on a single home server owned by its author and uses rclone and restic to store encrypted backups in the author's own Google Drive.

It requests only the drive.file scope: it can see and change only the files it creates itself. It is not offered to other users.

Herramienta personal de copias de seguridad: un servidor doméstico guarda copias cifradas en el Google Drive de su propio autor. Solo accede a los ficheros que ella misma crea.
